# Phase 7 — research-backed extension plan

Recorded 2026-09-27. This is a **plan, not an implementation or performance claim**. Preserve the current standalone decision-support boundary: NWIS cannot issue well-control instructions, infer field readiness from synthetic replay, or treat public Norwegian wells as OIL offsets. See the [implementation backlog](../phase-0/07-backlog.md) and [current demonstration gates](../phase-6/README.md).

## What the external scan actually establishes

The [StrataSense repository](https://github.com/SatyajeetChavan30/SIH_Pototype) lists formation-pick uncertainty, an alarm budget, a hash-chained log, a what-if planner, WITS-0/WITSML adapters, a rig-site view and Volve ingestion. Its file tree contains corresponding modules, but its README's AUC, data counts and operational assertions are **author claims**, not independently reproduced results. [DrillIntel](https://github.com/sudhanshu-0109/DrillIntel) explicitly separates regional research context from operational well records; its README's taxonomy is a design reference, not evidence that its ML scores or safeguards are validated. We have not verified a count of competing teams or any claim that a feature is unique.

OIL's [public eRTMAC tender](https://www.oil-india.com/files/oldtender/national/NIC_CDH6605P21.pdf) describes rig-to-centre WITS/WITSML data, a central repository, and sensor/operational-state-aware feed quality checks; an [OIL clarification](https://www.oil-india.com/files/oldtender/national/CORR6_CDH6605P21.pdf) says eRTMAC historical data would be stored in WITSML. Neither document grants NWIS access or specifies the deployed API/version, identifiers, authorization or latency. [Energistics](https://energistics.org/witsml-developers-users) recommends WITSML 2.1 with ETP 1.2 for new development; support OIL's *actual* version once confirmed, rather than assuming it follows that recommendation.

| 5 — eRTMAC adapter | Implement only against an approved OIL interface contract | Define a read-only inbound adapter contract for well/wellbore IDs, timestamp/time zone, MD/TVD/datum, units, channel codes, sensor quality and rig activity, sequence/cursor, backfill and gap behavior. | 6 — ML qualification | Train only after source-timed multiwell labels and reviewed no-event coverage exist | Freeze one hazard and forecast horizon; build pre-event windows, exclude post-onset leakage, split by well/field and time, compare nearest-well and simple-rule baselines, then assess calibration and alert burden. The order is about dependencies, not a promise to ship every row. Work packages 1–2 can be built without new OIL data; package 3 needs reviewed passages; package 5 needs OIL coordination; package 6 needs qualified labels and coverage.

## Stretch ideas with guardrails

- **Alarm budget:** First measure alerts per shift, missed critical events and suppression reasons on held-out replay. A configurable cap may digest lower-priority *advisories*, but must not silently suppress safety-critical events. Do not call a fixed cap “conformal prediction”: conformal coverage requires calibration assumptions that may fail under well/formation shift ([methodology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.13415)).
- **What-if planner:** Compare documented offset cases under user-entered scenarios as an *exploratory brief*. Do not output a safe mud-weight/ECD or casing recommendation until the required pressure, loss-window and engineering assumptions are reviewed. Record every assumption and show how results change when an offset is removed.
- **Mitigation graph:** Start as a traceable view over existing event, mitigation and outcome joins; include unknown effectiveness and case-mix caveats. A graph database or causal “what cured what” claim is unnecessary until links have been reviewed.
- **Voice memos:** First ship typed, attributed, peer-reviewed shift memos. Add optional on-device Assamese/Hindi/English transcription only with consent, retention rules, language-specific testing and a correction step. A transcript is not an approved drilling event.
- **Probabilistic ribbons / formation-pick uncertainty:** Research after reliable picks, survey datum and enough independent offsets exist. A Beta-Binomial interval needs a meaningful exposure denominator and comparable wells; it cannot turn a handful of heterogeneous reports into calibrated risk.

## Immediate next slice

1. Specify the provenance policy and its invariants against the existing schema (`dataset.qualification_status`, document drafts, approved events and alert evidence snapshots); prototype with synthetic fixtures only.
2. Draft the decision-ledger event contract and verifier, then implement it behind current alert/review transactions without changing user-facing recommendations.
3. In parallel with independent human review, prepare a *separate* held-out source registry and a claim-level scoring rubric. Do not rerun extraction into the approval-locked public staging database merely to improve a score.

Relevant standards and limits: [W3C PROV](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) distinguishes entities, activities, agents and derivation; [NIST's hash-chain definition](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/hash_chain) explains tamper evidence; [NIST log-management guidance](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/92/final) also requires operational protection and review of logs. These sources inform the design but do not certify NWIS.
